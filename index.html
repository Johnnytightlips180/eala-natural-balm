Our Story

The Éala Journey

♥

Éala began with a love for cosmetics that has been with me for as long as I can remember. As a child, I was always making rose petal perfumes and creating my own little lotions and potions. I never imagined then that this childhood fascination would one day lead me to creating my own skincare brand.

My Nanny has been a huge influence on my journey. She has always had a love for creams, balms and cosmetics, and that passion certainly rubbed off on me. Over the years, I began making creams and balms at home for myself and my family, while developing a growing interest in natural oils and ingredients. This is where the idea for Éala began — a simple, natural face balm for both men and women.

As I became more aware of what I was putting on my skin, I found myself drawn towards simpler formulations and carefully chosen ingredients. I had used Éala's four key ingredients individually over the years and loved incorporating them into my own skincare routine, so I wanted to bring them together in one beautiful balm.

As my passion grew, I decided to take the next step and develop Éala professionally. I completed the Making Natural Cosmetics and Ointments course at OBUS Academy, which gave me the knowledge and confidence to take my little idea and turn it into something real.

I always imagined my brand having an Irish name — something simple, beautiful and meaningful. Éala means “swan” in Irish, and the swan represents grace, beauty, peace, elegance and calm strength. To me, it felt like the perfect reflection of what I wanted Éala to be.

From a childhood love of making little lotions and potions, Éala was born.

Simple. Natural. Made with care.

Our Key Ingredients

♡

CASTOR OIL

Why we chose it for Éala

Helps moisturise and soften

Castor oil acts as an emollient, helping to leave the skin feeling soft, smooth and comfortably conditioned.

Rich and conditioning

Its naturally rich texture makes castor oil a beautiful addition to a balm designed to leave the skin feeling soft, comfortable and conditioned.

Part of Éala's distinctive texture

Castor oil works alongside the lighter oils and shea butter in Éala, helping to create the rich, easy-to-use balm texture that melts between your fingertips before application.

FRANKINCENSE OIL

Why we chose it for Éala

A beautiful natural aroma

Frankincense gives Éala its warm, woody and subtly sweet botanical scent. Its cosmetic function within the formulation is fragrance.

A traditional botanical ingredient

Frankincense has a long history of use in traditional cosmetic and aromatic preparations, making it a natural fit with the simple, carefully selected ingredients behind Éala.

Part of Éala's distinctive character

Used at the level assessed for the finished formulation, frankincense complements the oils and butter in the balm while contributing to its distinctive natural aroma and character.

JOJOBA OIL

Why we chose it for Éala

Soft and conditioning

Jojoba oil acts as an emollient, helping to leave the skin feeling soft, smooth and comfortably conditioned.

Light and silky

Its naturally silky texture makes jojoba a beautiful addition to facial skincare, providing a conditioned feel without leaving the skin feeling overly heavy.

Gives Éala its smooth glide

Jojoba works alongside the shea butter and castor oil in Éala, helping the balm spread smoothly and easily across the skin when warmed between your fingertips.

UNREFINED SHEA BUTTER

Why we chose it for Éala

Rich and conditioning

Shea butter acts as an emollient, helping to leave the skin feeling soft, smooth and comfortably conditioned.

Naturally creamy and nourishing

Its rich, creamy texture makes unrefined shea butter a beautiful foundation for Éala, contributing to the balm’s soft and luxurious feel.

The foundation of Éala’s texture

Shea butter provides the structure of the balm. Combined with jojoba and castor oils, it helps create Éala’s smooth, semi-solid texture that softens and melts between your fingertips before application.
